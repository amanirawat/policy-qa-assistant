# CISA Secure Our World

## Four simple steps to stay safe online
CISA's Secure Our World program recommends four basic steps: use strong passwords and a password manager, turn on multi-factor authentication, recognize and report phishing, and keep software updated.

## Strong passwords and password managers
CISA recommends that every password be long, random, and unique. A strong password is at least 16 characters long, and a different password should be used for every account so that one breach does not expose others. A password manager can generate, store, and fill strong passwords so people do not have to memorize them.

## Turning on multi-factor authentication (MFA)
Multi-factor authentication adds a second check, such as a code from an authenticator app or a security key, in addition to a password. CISA urges turning on MFA for email, banking, social media, and any account that offers it. Phishing-resistant MFA, such as FIDO security keys, is considered the strongest option.

## Recognizing phishing
Phishing messages try to trick people into clicking a malicious link, opening an infected attachment, or sharing passwords or financial information. Common warning signs include urgent or emotional language, requests for personal or financial information, unexpected attachments, offers that seem too good to be true, and sender email addresses or links that do not match the organization they claim to come from.

## What to do with a suspected phishing email
If an email looks suspicious, do not click its links, open its attachments, or reply with any information. Report it using your organization's phishing reporting button or by forwarding it to the IT or security team, then delete it. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, report it immediately and change the password for that account.

## Keeping software updated
Software updates often fix security vulnerabilities that attackers actively exploit. Turn on automatic updates for operating systems, browsers, and applications where possible, and install updates promptly when notified. Only download updates from official sources.

## Reporting incidents to CISA
Organizations can report cyber incidents, phishing, and vulnerabilities to CISA at report.cisa.gov or by calling CISA Central at 888-282-0870. Employees should follow their own organization's internal reporting process first.
